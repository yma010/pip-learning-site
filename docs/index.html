Learn with Pip

Pip Learning is a free iPhone app where kids practice English, math, science and history in short lessons, with hints from Pip the sea otter.

  • Kindergarten to grade 3 for now, matched to California and Arizona standards.
  • No account, no ads and nothing to buy. Mistakes never lock a lesson.
  • Everything your child does stays on the phone.
